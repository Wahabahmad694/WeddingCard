# Wahab Ahmad Weds Zarbab Mubeen — Royal Wedding Chronicle

A luxury, interactive, and animated wedding invitation website celebrating the wedding ceremonies of **Wahab Ahmad** and **Zarbab Mubeen**.

## Features

- ✉️ **Royal Wax Seal & 3D Envelope**: Monogrammed `W ♡ Z` seal with tap crackle, golden sparkle burst, and 3D card extraction.
- ✨ **Cinematic Golden Name Reveal**: Particle gathering and letter-by-letter shimmering calligraphy with Islamic blessings.
- ⏳ **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time counter ticking down to the big day (January 1st, 2027).
- 🌺 **Chronicle of Events**:
  - **Mehndi (Rasm-e-Hina)**: Friday, 1st Jan 2027 at DHA Rahbar Phase II, Lahore with full schedule & 12 sisters/cousins family badges.
  - **Barat (Wedding Ceremony)**: Saturday, 2nd Jan 2027 at Le Grand Chalet Royal, Johar Town, Lahore.
  - **Walima (Reception)**: Sunday, 3rd Jan 2027 at Marquesina Hall, Garrison Golf & Country Club, Saddar Town, Lahore.
- 🗺️ **One-Click Google Maps Navigation**: Direct map links and copy-address actions for all venues.
- 📅 **Add to Calendar**: Instant `.ics` calendar file download for each event.
- 🤲 **Interactive Blessings & Du'a Wall**: Guests can pick authentic Islamic prayers or write custom wishes with instant celebratory rose petal & confetti showers.
- 📞 **Direct Family Contact**: One-tap phone dialer and WhatsApp chat links for Waqas Ahmad and Mozam Naseer.
- 🌸 **Canvas Rose Petals & Golden Embers**: Realistic falling petals with breeze drift and toggle switch.
- 🎵 **Royal Ambient Music**: Ambient background wedding instrumental with play/mute controls.
- 📱 **Mobile & Desktop Optimized**: Responsive layout with smooth scroll snapping and quick jump navigation.

## Technologies Used

- **React 18 & Vite**
- **TypeScript**
- **Framer Motion 12**
- **Tailwind CSS 3**
- **Lucide Icons**
- **Sonner Toast Feedback**
